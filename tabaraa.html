Concept Project

Tabaraa

A humanitarian platform connecting donors with volunteering opportunities.

Overview

Tabaraa is a concept platform that centralizes donation and volunteering opportunities in one simple, warm, accessible place. The idea came from noticing how scattered and confusing charitable giving can feel online — Tabaraa gathers everything into one clear, human-centered experience.

The project focused on UI/UX design, information architecture, and building a visual identity that feels trustworthy but approachable.
