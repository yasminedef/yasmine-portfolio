Experience F1

A digital Formula 1 museum and ticketing platform.

Overview

Experience F1 is a digital Formula 1 museum and ticketing platform built as a university project. The site combines an immersive museum experience with a working ticketing system, letting fans explore the history of F1 and book event tickets in one place.

The project focused on structuring complex information clearly, designing an engaging visual identity inspired by motorsport, and building a smooth user flow from browsing the museum to purchasing a ticket.

It was developed as a team project, which strengthened my skills in collaboration, front-end development, and creative problem-solving.
